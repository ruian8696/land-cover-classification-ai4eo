This folder stores output figures generated by the land cover classification notebook.

The output files include:

- workflow_figure.png
  A workflow diagram summarising the Sentinel-2 land cover classification process.

- rgb_satellite_image.png
  A true-colour Sentinel-2 RGB image of the study area before classification.

- land_cover_map.png
  The final Random Forest land cover classification map.

- confusion_matrix.png
  A figure showing the confusion matrix used for accuracy assessment.

- feature_importance.png
  A plot showing the relative importance of Sentinel-2 bands and spectral indices in the Random Forest classifier.
