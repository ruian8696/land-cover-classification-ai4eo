This project does not store raw satellite imagery in this repository.

Sentinel-2 Surface Reflectance imagery is accessed directly through Google Earth Engine using the collection:

COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED

The study area is Central London, UK. The imagery is filtered by study area, date range, and cloud cover percentage inside the notebook.

Manual training polygons are created directly in the notebook and are not stored as separate shapefiles.
